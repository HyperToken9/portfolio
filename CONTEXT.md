# Nathan Saldanha — Portfolio

A personal portfolio site that presents Nathan's work to a broad range of readers: peers, recruiters and tech leads.

## Language

**Site structure**:
What the site shows, on which pages, and in what order, chosen to put the most important things in front of the reader first.
_Avoid_: Flow, layout (layout means spacing and visual arrangement, not what is shown or in what order)

**Experience**:
A place where Nathan did significant work (an employer, a client or a team), shown in order of priority. The place leads; the one or more pieces of work done there sit inside it, each leading to its own Case study.
_Avoid_: Featured work, professional experience, technical experience

**Case study**:
The full story of one piece of work done at an Experience: problem, approach, result and what was learned.
_Avoid_: Featured work, project page

**Side project**:
A smaller, hobby-scale project shown under "Just for fun", each with its own deep-dive page.
_Avoid_: Playground
