# Nathan Saldanha — Portfolio

A personal portfolio site that presents Nathan's work to a broad range of readers: peers, recruiters and tech leads.

## Language

**Site structure**:
What the site shows, on which pages, and in what order, chosen to put the most important things in front of the reader first.
_Avoid_: Flow, layout (layout means spacing and visual arrangement, not what is shown or in what order)

**Case study**:
One of the three featured projects, each given a full page with problem, approach, result and what was learned.
_Avoid_: Featured work, project page
